Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Pre-launch draft. Fields shown in yellow must be completed with AVERON's legal/business details before launch. This policy is intended for AVERON's online customers and should receive professional legal review before commercial launch in each market served.

1. Your statutory cancellation rights

For eligible online purchases, customers may have a statutory right to cancel or withdraw within the period provided by the consumer-protection laws applicable to their purchase, without giving a reason. If one order is delivered in separate shipments, the cancellation period generally runs from the day after the last item is received. After notifying us of cancellation, you generally have a further 14 days to send the goods back. These statutory rights are separate from, and are not reduced by, AVERON's voluntary 30-day return period.

2. AVERON 30-day returns

In addition to applicable statutory rights, AVERON accepts eligible change-of-mind return requests made within 30 days of delivery. This is a 30-day returns period, not a promise of free returns for every reason. You may inspect and try on clothing as you reasonably would in a shop. Items returned under AVERON's voluntary policy should otherwise be kept in a resalable condition, with original tags where reasonably possible.

3. Wrong size, colour or change of mind

If you ordered the wrong size or colour, or simply change your mind, you may request an eligible return within the applicable return period. Where the goods are not faulty and AVERON supplied what was ordered, you are responsible for the direct cost of returning the item, where the law permits. This return-cost responsibility is disclosed before purchase. Contact AVERON before sending anything so we can provide the correct return instructions and destination.

4. Faulty, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged, materially not as described, or AVERON sends the wrong product, size or colour, contact us promptly at [RETURNS EMAIL]. These issues are handled separately from change-of-mind returns. Where AVERON is legally responsible for the problem, we will provide the remedy required by applicable consumer law and will not make you bear return costs that legally belong to the seller. We may reasonably request photographs or other information needed to investigate the issue.

5. How to cancel or start a return

To exercise a statutory cancellation right, send AVERON a clear statement that you are cancelling the contract before the applicable deadline. You can do this by email to [RETURNS EMAIL]. For any return, include your order number, the item concerned and enough information for us to identify the purchase. A reason is helpful for service purposes but is not required for a valid statutory cancellation. We will provide the current return instructions and destination.

Do not send a return directly to CJdropshipping, a supplier, manufacturer or any address shown on the original parcel unless AVERON has specifically instructed you to do so. The supplier relationship does not limit your rights against AVERON.

7. Refund amount and original delivery

For a valid statutory cancellation, AVERON will reimburse the payments required by applicable law, including the cost of AVERON's least expensive standard outbound delivery option where legally required. If you selected a more expensive delivery method, the additional upgrade cost does not have to be reimbursed where the law permits. AVERON does not charge an arbitrary restocking or cancellation fee for exercising a statutory cancellation right.

8. Condition and handling of returned goods

You may inspect and try on clothing in a way comparable to what would reasonably be permitted in a shop. Where legally permitted, AVERON may reduce a refund to reflect diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods. This does not affect rights relating to faulty, incorrect or misdescribed goods.

9. Refund timing and payment method

Where the law requires a refund following cancellation, AVERON will reimburse you without undue delay and within the applicable legal deadline. For returned goods, AVERON may wait until the goods are received back or until you provide acceptable evidence that they have been sent back, where the law permits. Refunds are normally made using the same payment method used for the original transaction unless otherwise expressly agreed, and AVERON will not impose a refund fee.

10. Exchanges

AVERON does not guarantee direct size or colour exchanges. Where an exchange is offered, availability depends on current stock and fulfilment capability. We may instead process the eligible return and ask you to place a new order for the preferred size or colour. Nothing in this section limits statutory remedies for faulty, incorrect or misdescribed goods.

11. Exceptions

Certain categories of goods can be excluded from statutory change-of-mind cancellation rights, including some personalised or made-to-specification goods and certain sealed hygiene-sensitive goods after unsealing. AVERON will rely on an exception only where it genuinely applies and the required information has been provided to the customer. An exception to change-of-mind cancellation does not remove rights that apply when goods are faulty, incorrect or misdescribed.

12. Supplier and fulfilment arrangements

AVERON may use third-party fulfilment providers to prepare and ship orders. Any limitations in a supplier's own return process are an internal matter between AVERON and that supplier and do not reduce consumer rights that apply to the sale between AVERON and the customer.
