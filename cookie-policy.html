Pre-launch draft. Fields shown in yellow must be completed with AVERON's legal/business details before launch. This page is intended to reflect the current store setup and should receive professional legal review before commercial launch.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains cookies and other technologies that store information on, or access information from, your device. This includes browser local storage as well as traditional cookies.

2. Technologies currently used by AVERON

Strictly necessary / requested functionality

The current storefront uses browser local storage for functions such as keeping the shopping bag and wishlist available while you browse. The secure admin area also uses authentication/session technology. These functions are required to provide features you request or to secure the service.

Optional analytics and advertising

At the date of this draft, the supplied storefront does not intentionally load third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If AVERON adds tools such as analytics, advertising pixels or behavioural marketing later, this policy and the consent controls must be updated before those tools are enabled for users where consent is required.

3. Your choices

Where consent is required for a non-essential technology, it should remain disabled until you make an affirmative choice. You can reopen Cookie Settings to review or change your preference. Essential functionality cannot be disabled through this panel because it is needed to provide requested store features.

4. Third parties and duration

Before launch, AVERON should maintain a current internal inventory of every cookie, local-storage key, pixel, tag and similar technology, its provider, purpose and duration. Any third-party technology added later must also be reflected here.
