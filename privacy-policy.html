2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may process your name, email address, telephone number, billing and delivery address, order and transaction information, product selections, customer-service correspondence, device/browser information, IP address, security logs and consent preferences.

Payment card details are handled by our payment provider rather than being stored as full card details by AVERON.

3. Why we use your information

  • To take payment, process, fulfil and deliver orders and manage returns or refunds.
  • To provide customer support and communicate about an order.
  • To prevent fraud, secure the website and protect our legal rights.
  • To comply with tax, accounting and other legal obligations.
  • Where you choose to receive it, to send marketing communications. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Where consent is required, to use non-essential storage/access technologies such as analytics or advertising tools.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the activity, we rely on performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interests where those interests do not override your rights, and consent where the law requires it. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it.

5. Who receives information

We may share only the information necessary with service providers involved in operating the store, including payment processing (such as Stripe), order fulfilment and logistics providers (including CJdropshipping where used), hosting and infrastructure providers, professional advisers, fraud/security services, and public authorities where legally required.

6. International transfers

Some providers involved in payment, hosting, fulfilment or support may process information in other countries. Where applicable data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute requirements. Exact retention periods should be documented internally before launch. We use reasonable technical and organisational security measures, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing, request portability, and withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to the competent data-protection authority in your country or jurisdiction, where applicable. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] to make a request.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use browser storage and may use cookies or similar technologies. Essential technologies support functions such as the shopping bag, security and saved preferences. Non-essential technologies will be controlled according to your choices where consent is required. See our Cookie Policy.

10. Changes

We may update this notice as the store, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
